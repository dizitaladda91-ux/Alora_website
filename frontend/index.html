Frequently Asked Questions

Good To Know

Have questions about usage, ingredients, or daily routines? Find fast answers below.

Can I use the Alora Radiance Face Wash every day?

Yes! Our Face Wash is dermatologist-formulated to be gentle enough for daily use, morning and night, to remove impurities without stripping skin moisture.

Can I use the Face Serum every day?

Yes. Apply 3-4 drops daily after cleansing and before your face cream. If you are new to active Retinol, start with alternate nights.

When should I apply the Face Cream?

Apply the Face Cream right after your serum, morning and night, to lock in active ingredients and seal 24-hour hydration.

Can I use the Body Lotion every day?

Yes! Apply daily right after a bath/shower when skin is damp for maximum absorption and long-lasting velvety softness.

How often should I use the Face Scrub?

Use the Face Scrub 2–3 times a week to gently exfoliate dead skin cells, unclog pores, and restore skin smoothness.
