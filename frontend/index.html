Our Story

Meet Our Founder

At Alora Radiance, our story begins with Gurpreet Singh and a refined belief: skincare should feel honest, effective, and undeniably luxurious—without ever feeling complicated.

Gurpreet Singh recognized a gap in the market: many skincare products made bold promises, yet few delivered the elevated results and experience discerning customers expect. He set out to create a brand defined by trust, exceptional quality, and thoughtfully crafted formulations—one that would offer more than skincare, but a truly premium ritual. That vision became Alora Radiance.

What began as an idea soon evolved into a mission to create sophisticated skincare that integrates seamlessly into daily routines while still feeling exclusive, indulgent, and elevated. From the very beginning, every product has been developed with a commitment to transparency, performance, and a luxurious customer experience.

Today, Alora Radiance continues to grow with the same purpose that inspired its beginning: to bring high-end skincare to customers across the UAE, India, and beyond.

At Alora Radiance, every product reflects that journey—one shaped by trust, innovation, and the belief that radiant, healthy-looking skin deserves a truly premium experience.

– Gurpreet Singh
Founder, Alora Radiance

Good to Know

FAQs

Can I use the Alora Radiance Face Wash every day?

Yes. Our Face Wash is gentle enough for daily use, morning and night, to cleanse dirt, oil, and impurities.

Can I use the Face Serum every day?

Yes. Apply a few drops daily after cleansing and before your face cream. If you're new to retinol, begin with alternate nights.

When should I apply the Face Cream?

Use the Face Cream after your serum, morning and night, to lock in moisture and nourish your skin.

Can I use the Body Lotion every day?

Yes. Apply it daily, especially after a shower, for long-lasting hydration and soft, healthy-looking skin

How often should I use the Face Scrub?

Use the Face Scrub 2–3 times a week to gently exfoliate and remove dead skin cells.
