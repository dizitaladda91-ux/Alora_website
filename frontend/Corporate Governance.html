Corporate Governance

Effective Date: July 2026

At Alora Radiance, we believe that strong corporate governance is the foundation of a trustworthy, responsible, and sustainable business. Our governance framework is designed to ensure ethical business practices, transparency, accountability, regulatory compliance, and long-term value for our customers, business partners, employees, and stakeholders.

1. Our Commitment

Alora Radiance is committed to conducting business with honesty, integrity, fairness, and professionalism. Every business decision is guided by ethical principles that promote customer trust, responsible operations, and sustainable business growth.

2. Ethical Business Practices

We maintain high ethical standards across all aspects of our operations. We encourage honesty, transparency, respect, and accountability in every interaction with customers, suppliers, employees, and business partners. Fraudulent, misleading, or unethical practices are not tolerated.

3. Transparency

We strive to provide accurate and transparent information regarding our products, pricing, policies, and services. Customers should have access to clear information that enables informed purchasing decisions without hidden charges or misleading claims.

4. Compliance with Laws

Alora Radiance complies with all applicable laws, regulations, industry standards, and legal requirements governing our business operations. We regularly review our practices to ensure continued compliance with evolving legal obligations.

5. Customer Protection

Protecting customer interests is a core principle of our business. We are committed to providing safe products, secure payment processing, transparent return policies, and responsive customer support to maintain customer confidence and satisfaction.

6. Data Privacy & Security

We recognize the importance of protecting personal information. Appropriate technical and organizational safeguards are implemented to help protect customer data from unauthorized access, misuse, disclosure, or loss. Personal information is handled in accordance with our Privacy Policy.

7. Responsible Supply Chain

We seek to work with suppliers and business partners who share our commitment to ethical sourcing, quality standards, fair labor practices, environmental responsibility, and legal compliance. We encourage responsible business conduct throughout our supply chain.

8. Product Quality

Maintaining consistent product quality is one of our highest priorities. We continuously evaluate our products, manufacturing standards, and quality assurance processes to ensure customers receive reliable and high-quality beauty and skincare products.

9. Environmental Responsibility

Alora Radiance recognizes its responsibility toward environmental sustainability. We continually explore opportunities to reduce waste, improve operational efficiency, encourage responsible packaging practices, and support environmentally conscious business decisions wherever practical.

10. Employee Responsibility

We foster a workplace built on respect, equal opportunity, diversity, professionalism, and mutual accountability. Employees are expected to follow company policies, uphold ethical standards, and contribute positively to the organization's culture.

11. Risk Management

We actively identify, assess, and manage operational, financial, technological, and regulatory risks that may affect our business. Continuous monitoring and internal controls help strengthen business resilience and protect stakeholder interests.

12. Continuous Improvement

We believe corporate governance is an evolving process. Our policies, operational procedures, and governance practices are periodically reviewed and improved to reflect changing regulations, customer expectations, and industry best practices.

13. Policy Updates

Alora Radiance reserves the right to revise this Corporate Governance Policy at any time. Updated versions will become effective immediately upon publication on this website. Users are encouraged to review this page periodically for any updates.

14. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding our Corporate Governance practices, ethical standards, or company policies, please contact our customer support through the Contact Us page available on the Alora Radiance website. We value transparency and welcome your feedback.
