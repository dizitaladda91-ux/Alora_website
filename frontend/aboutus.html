Meet Our Founder

At Alora Radiance, our story begins with Gurpreet Singh and a refined belief: skincare should feel honest, effective, and undeniably luxurious—without ever feeling complicated.

Gurpreet recognized a gap in the market: many skincare products made bold promises, yet few delivered the elevated results and experience discerning customers expect. He set out to create a brand defined by trust, exceptional quality, and dermatologist-proven formulations.
